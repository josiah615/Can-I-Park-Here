# Product

**Can I Park Here?** reads a photo of the parking signs on a street pole and tells a driver, in one glance, whether they can park for their whole planned stay.

- **User:** a driver standing next to the pole, phone in one hand, often double-parked. Wants an answer in under 10 seconds.
- **Job:** "Tell me yes or no for my stay, and when I have to move."
- **Platform:** mobile web first; desktop works too.
- **Tone:** plain, direct, calm. Never cute. Errors say what to do next.
- **Trust:** always show the rule that decided the answer and a confidence level; low confidence becomes CAN'T TELL with a better-photo tip. The posted sign is the final authority.
- **Out of scope:** finding open spots, payments, ticket disputes, accounts.
